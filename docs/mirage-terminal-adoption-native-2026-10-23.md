# Native terminal Mirage adoption and quota release

Root-reported native evidence,2026-10-23. This document was written without
reading private snapshots or controlling the client. The existing abandoned
Mirage attempt was adopted into an explicitly LOCAL re-entry series through
the normal native Retry flow. **No battle occurred during this adoption check.**
An earlier E6 probe and loss are separate evidence and are not attributed to
these new Mirage generations.

## Configuration and read-only boot

The compatible170-package profile/stamp1634693, local team curve,2021 Rupee
mode/cap3, resource snapshots, paid recruit target049 and +15 instrumented APK
were retained. The new adoption option was explicitly `terminal-local`, with
the matching configured re-entry policy. Its daily allowance3, UTC day boundary,
free re-entry and abandonment fallback remain authored LOCAL policy, not
recovered original assignment, price, quota or reset law.

After boot, the first native Mirage GetInfo left schema12 intact with no
`mirageSeries`. The original abandoned run and all three complete saved games
were exact. Read-only startup/hydration therefore did not create an attempt,
heal owned Hunters or consume allowance. The native panel exposed Retry.

## Native capture, reset and exhaustion

First Retry issued GetNextFloor followed by the native same-floor GetInfo
reset/stay request. The resulting schema14 series reached generation2,
used2, day20749. It archived the existing attempt as:

```json
{"generation":1,"floor":1,"begin_ltime":1792756155,"claimed":[1,2],"abandoned":true,"completed":false}
```

The old claims and actual entry time were retained, not replaced with adoption
time or converted into a completed floor. The new run had stair1/state1,
five enrolled owned families at Mirage HP10000 and an empty separate party.
The old COMPLETE map markers were gone under the fresh captured run. This does
not imply that later stairs were unlocked before earning them. No RegisterDeck
or battle was started to obtain this observation.

A second Retry, including Yes on the active-attempt warning, produced
generation3/used3. Its archived generation2 retained begin1792762016,
claims[], abandonedfalse and completedfalse. This was an intentionally
unfinished attempt replaced through the configured native re-entry workflow;
it was not a second floor clear or a hidden battle result.

A third Retry reached the configured cap. It abandoned the current generation3
without creating generation4: state0, empty entry/party and all11 owned Hunters'
Mirage HP returned to the historical1000 placeholder. Native UI showed a
countdown of approximately10hours28minutes to the next UTC day. That display
is evidence for this configured clock projection, not an original quota law.
The active-warning and same-floor chains were exercised natively; no host
gameplay request or save edit supplied the release.

## Persisted-state audit and limits

Root's comparison found every main-game field exact apart from AP/BP clock
fields; both other saved games were exact. Main-account OTP/lastLoginAt were
the only account differences. The Rupee purchase journal remained exact.
Gold18700, free Manda239635 and paid Manda240000 were unchanged. The new
schema14 run/series/history is separate durable journal data; this does not
mean the entire state file remained byte-identical.

The adoption trace contains five Mirage GetInfo requests and three
GetNextFloor requests, with no End, Reward, RegisterDeck or BattleStart.
Consequently this check proves native adoption/reset/quota exhaustion/roster
release, not combat, new rewards, next-day renewal or a full-floor victory.
The previously observed misleading COMPLETE labels after abandonment remain
a presentation limitation; a fresh generation's cleared markers disappearing
does not retroactively complete the old run.

The cold server/client reload also passed with adoption **disabled** and the
identical captured re-entry policy retained. The normal loader restored
schema14, and native Mirage entry showed the exhausted countdown again
(approximately10hours22minutes at the later observation). The complete run,
series/history and all three saved games were exact against the pre-reload
snapshot. Only main OTP/lastLoginAt changed; all Rupee counters remained exact.
The reload trace contains three GetInfo reads and no GetNextFloor, so it did
not create or consume another attempt. The one-time adoption option remains
disabled in the final live process; matching configured re-entry remains on.

The client returned safely to Village with no battle/helper. Final observed
runtime: listener42732/session67901, native client27060, same +15 APK.
JDWP forwarding must be rebound before any later observer; earlier client
PIDs in the control measurements are historical.

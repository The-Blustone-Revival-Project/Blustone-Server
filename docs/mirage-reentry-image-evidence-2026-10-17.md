  per day, every00:00. JA/SC/TC also state once daily at midnight. Clicking

- Row159, `CONTENTS9_5`: the **Mirage Market** refreshes at midnight and permits
  at most3 purchased refreshes. That3-use shop limit is not a run-reentry
  allowance. These are separate help records and transactions.

## Exclusions and remaining numerical gaps

`batch-70.tsv:43`, idx3492 explicitly mentions midnight00:00 UTC+9 for a
**water-bottle event** with four task rewards per day, including clearing
Abyss once. Its event timezone and4-reward cap cannot establish the Mirage
run's timezone or quota. Source SHA256
`08b79c5d66a7cfef3d34fdbd9a72a122731724891b9960753f0a0a5a3d78520e`;
raw-row SHA256
`5da8d042f9428b2a396f870222ab100a357455c63134df9f3ed8392fc1c41ed1`.
Missing-Hunter Search re-entry countdowns and Forest of Mirage expedition
Retry results are different systems and cannot price/reset Ruins.

No inspected source supplies run paid resets, a retry price, timezone, floor
delta, backtrack reward multiplier, saved allowance field or precise meaning
of choosing Stay relative to consuming the daily allowance. A configured
repeat policy must label those decisions LOCAL instead of importing the
Market3-refresh rule or the separate event's UTC+9 clock.
